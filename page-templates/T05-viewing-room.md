# T05 — Viewing Room Page

**File slot:** computed per Canon Resolution §3 (slug `viewing-room`; sequenced directly after the catalog page) *(Canon §6 erratum, item 9: fixed numeral replaced by computed slot)*
**Existing components:** `lesson-hero`, `lesson-wrap`, `visual-card`, gallery grids
**Institutional model:** Judging programs (4-H, breed associations) train identification through many varied real examples, not one textbook image per type. The research mechanism is interleaved category learning: mixed-order exemplars beat grouped exemplars for identifying *new* examples (59% vs. 36%, Kornell & Bjork 2008).

**Required for:** every course whose quiz asks learners to identify something visually (colors, markings, patterns, breeds, tack, anatomy regions). Courses currently missing a Viewing Room for visual subjects (e.g., hoof anatomy) should have one added — this is the highest-value structural gap found in the audit.

## Required sections (in order)

1. **Purpose line** — one sentence: what the learner should be able to recognize after this page.
2. **Exemplar gallery** — minimum 3 photos per catalog item, **arranged in mixed order across items, never grouped by item.** Each photo: caption ≤ 20 words naming the item, alt text describing the distinguishing feature.
3. **Contrast pairs** — side-by-side panels for every commonly confused pair (star/snip, bay/brown, sock/stocking), with the separating feature visually signaled (arrow or highlight) and named in one line.
4. **Spot-It self-check** — 3–5 "Which one is this?" prompts using photos **not** shown in the gallery above. Reveal answer + one-line reason on interaction. Ungraded.
5. **Continue CTA** to Training Barn.

## Content rules

- Variation within each item is the point: different horses, angles, lighting, coat conditions. Three near-identical photos teach less than three varied ones.
- Mixed-order arrangement is mandatory. If the page currently groups all examples of one item together, reorder — no new assets required.
- Minimal text. This page is for eyes, not paragraphs: no section over 60 words.

## Do not

- Reuse Spot-It photos in the module quiz (the quiz also needs novel images).
- Introduce any item not on the catalog page.
- Group the gallery by item "for tidiness" — grouping is the documented worse condition.
