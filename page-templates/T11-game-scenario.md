# T11 — Scenario Challenge

**Covers existing games:** color-scenarios, vitals-challenge, vitals-methods, color-challenge, anatomy-challenge (scenario rounds), quiz-corral standalone challenges
**Skill level:** Analysis/Evaluation (Bloom: Analyze–Evaluate) — top rung; required at Schooling Ring and Learning Loft, optional at Round Pen
**Institutional model:** Horse judging's oral reasons and 4-H skillathon scenario stations — the learner applies knowledge to a realistic situation and justifies a decision. This is how every reviewed institution bridges from identification to judgment.

Learner reads a short barn-world scenario, makes a decision, and sees expert reasoning.

## Required structure

1. **Instruction line** (≤ 25 words).
2. **Scenario stem** (40–80 words, third-person) — a concrete situation: "A boarder reports her gelding's resting heart rate at 52 bpm after evening feed…"
3. **Decision point** — one question with 3–4 options. Options are plausible courses of action or interpretations, not one obvious answer plus jokes.
4. **Expert reasoning feedback** — for *every* option (chosen or revealed): why it is or isn't right, in 2–3 sentences referencing taught facts. This mirrors judging reasons: the explanation is the learning event.
5. **Set length** — 4–8 scenarios per challenge.
6. **Summary** — decisions correct, replay + onward links.

## Content rules

- Scenarios use only taught content plus common sense; health scenarios must align with AAEP guidance and never resolve with "call the vet is unnecessary" framing — when in doubt, the right option involves consulting a professional.
- Difficulty scales by tier: Round Pen scenarios have one clearly best answer; Learning Loft scenarios may have two defensible answers where the feedback compares them (preserves real-world judgment nuance).
- Third-person scenario prose; imperative only in instructions.

## Do not

- Invent statistics, dosages, or medical specifics not verified in course content.
- Write trick questions — difficulty comes from discrimination, not deception.

## QA checklist

- [ ] 4–8 scenarios, 40–80 word stems
- [ ] Feedback written for every option
- [ ] Health content AAEP-safe, vet-positive
- [ ] Tier-appropriate ambiguity
