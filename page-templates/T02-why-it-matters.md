# T02 — Why It Matters / Orientation Page

**File slot:** computed per Canon Resolution §3 (slug `why-<topic>` or `what-is-<topic>`) *(Canon §6 erratum, item 9: fixed numeral replaced by computed slot)*
**Existing components:** `lesson-hero`, `lesson-wrap`, `visual-card`
**Institutional model:** Every extension fact sheet opens with a relevance paragraph before content; 4-H project books open each activity with a real barn situation; Gagné events 1 & 3 (gain attention, activate prior knowledge). This page exists in every current Equine EDU course — keep it; it matches best practice.

Two variants share this template:
- **Why variant** (`why-markings-matter`) — for topics learners may not see the point of yet.
- **What-is variant** (`what-is-driving`) — for topics where the discipline itself needs introducing first. Same structure; the hook explains the activity instead of the stakes.

## Required sections (in order)

1. **Hook** (50–100 words) — one concrete barn-world scenario, question, or surprising fact. A situation the learner can picture ("Two bay geldings stand side by side at a show check-in…") beats an abstract claim ("Markings are important.").
2. **Recall prompt** (1–3 sentences) — ties to the previous module in the subject path, or to everyday experience for module 1. Phrase as a prompt to think, not a quiz.
3. **What this module covers** (50–100 words) — module objectives restated in plain language: "By the end of this module, learners will be able to…"
4. **Where it leads** (1–2 sentences) — how this knowledge is used next: the following course, horse bowl, judging, or daily horse care.
5. **Continue CTA** — one button to page 3.

## Content rules

- Word count: 200–350 total.
- Introduce **zero** new terminology here. If a term is unavoidable in the hook, use the everyday word instead.
- One image maximum, and it must serve the hook scenario.

## Do not

- Start teaching content — that is page 3's job.
- Use second person in prose. The scenario is third-person ("a rider notices…", not "you notice…").

## QA checklist

- [ ] Hook is a concrete scenario, not a claim
