# T23 — Fun Quiz (Quiz Corral)

**Status:** Adopted — the Quiz Corral's page kind
**Placement:** Quiz Corral only. Fun quizzes are engagement and organic-traffic content; they carry **no assessment role, no scoring records, no course-progress meaning.** That separation protects the low-stakes integrity of T07 (the only assessment) and keeps the Corral's tone free.
**Purpose (dual):** (1) "just for fun" content for existing learners; (2) shareable, searchable entry points that bring new horse-curious visitors into the platform. Every fun quiz ends with a path into a real course — the Corral is a front porch, not a dead end.
**Institutional model:** This page kind has no equivalent in 4-H/USPC/extension — it is a web-content convention (quiz-style engagement content), and is flagged as such: the justification is engagement and acquisition, not learning science. The learning-science contribution is indirect but real: a "How many colors can you identify?" challenge is retrieval practice in disguise, and reusing course image pools makes the fun version instructionally honest.

## Two formats

**A. Personality / match quiz** — no right answers. "What riding style fits you best?", "What kind of horse suits you?"
**B. Challenge / score quiz** — right answers, playful framing. "How many of these 12 colors can you identify?", "Can you name these saddle parts?"

## Required structure

1. **Title** — phrased as the question people would search or click ("What Riding Style Fits You Best?"). The title is the SEO asset; write it before the quiz.
2. **Intro** (40–80 words) — playful but on-voice: warm, family-friendly, no hype. Sets expectations: how many questions, what you get at the end.
3. **Questions** — 6–12.
   - *Format A:* situational preference questions, 3–4 answer options, each mapped to a result profile. No option is mocked or clearly "wrong."
   - *Format B:* same construction rules as course practice — near-miss distractors, interleaved order, images from the shared pools where applicable.
4. **Result page** — the conversion moment:
   - *Format A:* result profile (e.g., "Western trail riding") with a 60–100 word friendly description, an honest "also consider…" secondary match, and a CTA to the matching course ("Learn what makes Western riding unique → Common Riding Styles").
   - *Format B:* score with an encouraging band label (no failing framing — "4/12: Every horseperson started here"), the answers revealed with one-line identifiers, and a CTA to the course that teaches the missed content.
5. **Share affordance** — result/score is shareable (image card or link). The share asset shows the quiz title and result, never a learner's identity beyond what they choose to share.
6. **More-quizzes strip** — 2–3 other Corral quizzes, then the Corral hub link.

## Content rules

- Format A results must be genuinely informative — real characteristics of the discipline/breed type, registry- and association-accurate, written so a newcomer learns something true from a fun result. No horoscope-vacuous results.
- Format B answers use catalog wording verbatim and may draw from the shared novel-image pool; they must never *contradict* course content or teach untaught "gotcha" facts.
- Voice: the Corral may relax further than lesson prose — second person **is allowed here** ("you", "your result"), since this is conversational web content, not lesson instruction. Everything else in the voice rules (family-friendly, no hype, no emoji) still applies.
- Health topics stay out of Format A entirely (no "diagnose your horse" fun quizzes); Format B health content follows AAEP-aligned course facts only.
- SEO basics per quiz: descriptive URL slug, meta description, one H1 matching the title, image alt text.

## Do not

- Award badges, store scores, or imply course credit.
- Gate results behind signup walls (a soft "save your result / start learning" prompt is fine; forced capture kills shareability and trust).
- Let Format B difficulty exceed what a course actually teaches — frustration is not a conversion strategy.
- Cross the streams: no Horse Bowl, T07, or study-bank content branding inside the Corral, and no fun quizzes inside course flows.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Title is the search phrase
- [ ] Every result/score page has exactly one course CTA
- [ ] Format A results registry/association-accurate; Format B answers match catalog wording
- [ ] Share asset works without exposing identity
- [ ] No scores stored, no gating
