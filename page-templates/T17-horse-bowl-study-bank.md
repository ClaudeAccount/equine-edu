# T17 — Horse Bowl Study Bank

**Status:** Adopted — site-level resource, not a course page
**Placement:** Learning Loft (the cross-course study hub), with per-course feeders: each course's T16 "Horse Bowl focus" section supplies entries. Lives outside courses; links back into them. **Not the Quiz Corral** — the Corral is the gamified fun-quiz space (T23) and carries no assessment or competition-prep role; serious study tools and fun quizzes stay visibly separate so neither dilutes the other.
**Institutional model:** This is how the discipline actually works — state 4-H Horse Bowl programs train from published question banks organized by topic and source, with difficulty rounds. The bank format *is* the institutional standard; Equine EDU's addition is linking every question to the lesson that teaches it. Research basis: question banks are structured retrieval practice with spacing built in (Roediger & Karpicke; Dunlosky), and cumulative mixed-topic rounds add interleaving (Kornell & Bjork).

**Both inside and outside courses** (the research question's answer): focus areas live inside courses (T16); the bank itself is one site-level system. Duplicating full banks per course would fragment maintenance and break the single-source rule.

## Required structure

1. **Bank home** (a T13 collection-hub variant, in the Learning Loft) — subjects as cards, each showing question count and tier coverage; "Mixed Round" CTA front and center.
2. **Question entries** — fixed anatomy:
   - Question text (horse-bowl phrasing: short, factual, one correct answer)
   - Answer (+ acceptable alternates)
   - Category: Quick Recall / Identification (image) / Applied Knowledge / Challenge
   - Tier tag: Round Pen / Schooling Ring / Learning Loft
   - Source link: the Equine EDU page that teaches it (learner-visible), plus the internal verification source (writer-only, per source-presentation rules)
3. **Round modes:**
   - *Practice round* — 10–20 questions, chosen subject(s) + tier, untimed, feedback per question.
   - *Mixed round* — cross-subject, interleaved, the default recommendation.
   - *Buzzer round* (Learning Loft) — timed recall to mirror competition pressure. The only timed experience on the platform; clearly labeled as competition simulation.
4. **Difficulty scaling** — category ladder maps to tiers: Quick Recall/ID dominate Round Pen banks; Applied and Challenge appear from Schooling Ring; Challenge questions (multi-step, "name all four…") are Learning Loft.
5. **Missed-question review** — end-of-round list with answers and lesson links; option to replay only missed items (successive relearning).

## Content rules

- Every question must trace to a taught Equine EDU page **and** a verifiable authority (extension publication, AAEP, breed registry, AYHC-style references). Unverifiable questions don't enter the bank.
- Answers use catalog/glossary wording verbatim.
- No trick questions; competition difficulty comes from precision and speed, not deception.
- Identification questions use the shared novel-image pool (with T19), never Viewing Room images.

## Do not

- Build per-course mini-banks as separate pages.
- Let bank questions drift from course wording — when a course updates, its bank entries update in the same change.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Every entry: question, answer, category, tier, lesson link, internal source
- [ ] Category mix appropriate to tier
- [ ] Mixed rounds interleave subjects
- [ ] Buzzer mode labeled as competition simulation, opt-in
