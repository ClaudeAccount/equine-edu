Research basis: practice with immediate feedback must precede scored assessment (Gagné events 6–7), and retrieval practice beats restudy (Roediger & Karpicke).

The Training Barn is the course's **access point into the Loft Training Barn** (Canon S1): its activity cards launch the single site-level game instances (T08–T11, T19) scoped to this course, plus the downloads page (T12). It is unscored, unlimited-attempt territory.

## Required sections (in order)

1. **Barn intro** (≤ 50 words, imperative voice allowed) — what to do here, that nothing is scored, attempts are unlimited, and the quiz comes next.
2. **Activity cards** — 2–4 cards, ordered easiest → hardest. Each card: type tag (Match / Sort / Label / Challenge), name, one-line description of what the learner practices (≤ 15 words), time estimate, launch button.
3. **Review mix note** (from the 2nd course in a subject path onward) — one card or one activity must include items from the previous course (spaced retrieval).
4. **Downloads card** — links to the worksheet/printables page (T12) if the course has one.
5. **Continue CTA** to Test Your Knowledge.

## Content rules

- Recommended activity ladder per course: one **Match** (recognition) → one **Sort or Label** (application) → one **Challenge/Scenario** (judgment).
