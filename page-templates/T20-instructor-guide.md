# T20 — Instructor Guide

**Status:** Adopted — **Future Phase** (spec frozen now; build when the Instructor Area ships). Do not create learner-visible placeholders.
**Placement:** Future Instructor Area, one guide per course, linked from (not embedded in) the course.
**Institutional model:** Universal convention across every program reviewed — 4-H project leader guides, FFA CDE coach materials, CHA instructor manuals, USPC instruction guides, and CTE curricula all ship a facilitator document alongside learner content. No additional learning-science claim is needed: this is an access-and-adoption feature for the instructor/parent/club-leader audience, and the convention is unanimous.

**Public or restricted (the research question):** guides public — open guides drive adoption by 4-H leaders, homeschool parents, and barn instructors, matching extension practice where leader guides are freely published. Quiz answer rationales and any future gradebook features: restricted to instructor accounts. Worksheet answer keys remain public (they already ship in T12).

## Required structure (one guide per course)

1. **Course snapshot** — outcomes (verbatim from T01), tier, page count, learner time estimate, group-session time estimate (typically 1.5–2× solo time).
2. **Materials list** — printables from T12, plus physical options for in-person settings ("real halter or bridle if available").
3. **Session plan** — suggested 30–60 minute group walkthrough mapping course pages to a meeting agenda: hook discussion (T02) → content walkthrough (T03/T04) → group activity (a T08–T11/T19 game projected, or its printable analog) → exit quiz (T07 together or individually).
4. **Discussion prompts** — 4–6 open questions per course, tiered (recall → application → opinion-with-reasons, mirroring Bloom and oral-reasons practice).
5. **Group activity suggestions** — 2–3 offline adaptations of the course's digital games (barn scavenger hunt for T10 content; flashcard relay for T08).
6. **Watch-fors** — the course's Common Mistakes list (from T16) annotated with how the misconception shows up in person and how to correct it.
7. **Extension & differentiation** — one step down (supports for younger members) and one step up (Horse Bowl prep tie-in via T17; judging reasons practice via T11) per course.
8. **Assessment notes** — what the T07 quiz covers, retake policy, how to use results to target review.

## Content rules

- Instructor-facing voice may address the instructor as "you" — the third-person rule applies to learner prose only.
- Guides restate learner content by reference (links/page numbers), never by copying — single source of truth.
- Internal source citations may appear here (instructors benefit from knowing the authority), unlike learner pages.

## Do not

- Build guides before the Instructor Area exists (no orphaned public pages).
- Let guides drift: a guide updates in the same change as its course.

## QA checklist

- [ ] Outcomes verbatim from T01
- [ ] Session plan maps to actual course pages
- [ ] Discussion prompts tiered
- [ ] No copied learner content
