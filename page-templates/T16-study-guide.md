# T16 — Study Guide / Review Page

> **Canon errata applied (Canon Resolution Phase 0 v1.0 §6, items 11 & 12).** File slot is computed per Canon §3 (slug `study-guide`, between T06 and T07). Tier references use the canonical name **Loft tier** (Canon §2.2).

**Status:** Adopted (modified from proposal) — retrieval-oriented, not a reread sheet
**File slot:** computed per Canon Resolution §3 (slug `study-guide`, between Training Barn and Test Your Knowledge); always also exported as a printable in T12 *(Canon §6 erratum, item 11)*
**Institutional model:** USPC certification prep sheets and CHA level-test review. Research caveat that shaped this spec: passive review sheets and rereading are *low-utility* techniques, while practice testing and spaced retrieval are the two highest-utility techniques reviewed (Dunlosky et al. 2013). A study guide earns its place only if every section makes the learner retrieve, then check — successive relearning improved real exam scores by more than a letter grade versus business-as-usual review (Rawson & Dunlosky; Janes et al. 2020).

**Required or optional:** Recommended for all courses; **required** at Loft tier and for any course feeding a Horse Bowl bank. Round Pen courses may rely on the Training Barn + printable study sheet alone.

## Required structure (in order)

1. **How to use this page** (≤ 40 words) — try each prompt before revealing; revisit tomorrow ("twice, on different days" is the explicit instruction — spacing).
2. **Key Concepts as prompts** — each major concept phrased as a question with a reveal ("What are the five common face markings, from smallest to largest?"), not as a restated paragraph. 5–10 prompts.
3. **Key Vocabulary** — embedded T15 component (or a term-list with reveals if the component isn't available).
4. **Spot-check gallery** (visual courses) — 4–6 novel images with reveal answers; pulls from the T19 image pool.
5. **Common Mistakes** — 3–5 "people often confuse X with Y" items, each stating the distinguishing rule. Mirrors the confusable callouts in T04 and the near-miss logic in T07.
6. **Horse Bowl focus** (Schooling Ring and Learning Loft) — the 5–8 facts from this course most likely to appear in competition, phrased as quick-recall Q→A reveals. These feed the T17 bank.
7. **Ready check + CTA** — "Able to answer all prompts without peeking? Take the quiz." Links to T07 and back to Training Barn.

## Content rules

- Every item is prompt → reveal. Zero sections of plain restated prose. If a writer can't phrase it as a question, it doesn't belong here.
- All answers use catalog-page wording verbatim (single source of truth).
- Length: 1 screen-scroll target; 10–20 total prompts. This is a sharpening stone, not a second lesson.

## Do not

- Produce a summary narrative or "everything we covered" wall of text — that's the documented low-utility format.
