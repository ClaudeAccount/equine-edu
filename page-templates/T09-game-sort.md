# T09 — Sort & Classify Game

**Covers existing games:** color-sort, style-sort, region-sort
**Skill level:** Application/Analysis (Bloom: Apply) — second rung of the Training Barn ladder
**Institutional model:** Hippology station classes ("place each item in its group") and judging class organization; mechanism is category discrimination — deciding *which group* forces attention to distinguishing features (Kornell & Bjork, discriminative contrast).

Learner drags items (photos or terms) into 2–4 labeled bins (e.g., face vs. leg markings; English vs. Western vs. Driving; forehand vs. barrel vs. hindquarters).

## Required structure

1. **Instruction line** (imperative, ≤ 25 words): "Drag each photo into the correct group."
2. **Bins** — 2–4 categories, each labeled with the exact catalog/group name. More than 4 bins overloads the round; split into two rounds instead.
3. **Item pool** — 8–14 items, shuffled, drawn from the catalog. Include at least two "near-miss" items that sit close to a bin boundary (a tall sock that looks like a stocking).
4. **Feedback per drop** — correct: brief confirm + identifier. Incorrect: item returns, with the relevant distinguishing rule shown ("Stockings extend above the knee or hock").
5. **Round summary** — items correct, the rule(s) most missed, replay + onward links.

## Content rules

- Bin names and item names must match catalog wording exactly.
- The near-miss items are the instructional payload — every round needs at least two.
- Keyboard-operable alternative required (select item → select bin) for accessibility.

## Do not

- Use bins the catalog page never defined.
- Make rounds longer than 14 items — fatigue, not learning.

## QA checklist

- [ ] 2–4 bins, exact catalog names
- [ ] ≥ 2 near-miss items per round
- [ ] Miss feedback states the distinguishing rule
- [ ] Keyboard path works
