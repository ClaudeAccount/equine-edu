Upload three horse comparison photos to this folder:

  chestnut-compare.jpg   — A chestnut horse (full body or side profile, good lighting)
  black-compare.jpg      — A black horse (same crop/angle as chestnut if possible)
  bay-compare.jpg        — A bay horse (same crop/angle)

Tips for best slider results:
- Same image dimensions for all three (e.g. 1200×800)
- Side profile or 3/4 view works best
- Good lighting so coat color reads clearly
- Similar background across all three if possible

Once uploaded, the slider placeholders on comparing-base-colors.html
will automatically swap in the real photos — no code changes needed.
