# Equine Edu — Product Brief
*Last updated: May 2026*

---

## What Equine Edu Is

Equine Edu is a modern equine education platform built to make horse learning structured, approachable, and genuinely engaging. It is not a blog, a forum, or a traditional horse website. It is a digital learning ecosystem — closer in feel to a premium online learning platform than anything currently available in equine education.

The platform is built and authored by a BSc-qualified equestrian science professional with over 20 years of industry experience. That credential is a core differentiator. The content depth, scenario accuracy, and judgment calls embedded in the platform are only possible because of that foundation.

**The product in three sentences:**
> Learn through courses. Compete in Horse Bowl. Care for your virtual horse.

---

## Who It's For

Equine Edu is for **all learners** — not a specific age group, not club members, not existing horse owners. The platform serves:

- Complete beginners with no horse experience
- Horse enthusiasts who want structured, accurate knowledge
- Students supplementing riding lessons with theoretical understanding
- Youth learners (4-H, Pony Club adjacent, FFA)
- Adult learners returning to or newly discovering horses
- Anyone who wants to understand horses more clearly and confidently

**You do not need to own a horse. You do not need barn access.** The platform is designed so anyone, anywhere can build genuine equine knowledge.

---

## Business Model

Membership-based subscription platform. All core content accessible through a subscription tier. Future development will include subscription infrastructure once the front-end foundation is complete.

## The Three Pillars

### 1. Courses
**Purpose: Structured learning and confidence building.**

Courses are the knowledge foundation of the platform. They are non-competitive, self-paced, and designed to feel premium and approachable — not gamified in a way that creates pressure or anxiety.

**Gamification elements:**
- Progress bars — visual completion tracking per course
- Learning streaks — rewards consistency without punishing missed days
- Completion certificates — per course, downloadable/shareable
- Achievement badges — tied to learning milestones within a course
- **Mastery badges** — earned by completing a full topic area

**Mastery badge categories (examples, grows with content):**
- Hoof Basics
- Face Markings
- Leg Markings
- Common Colors
- Barn Safety
- Horse Senses
- Parts of the Horse
- *(each new course topic becomes a new badge)*

Mastery badges live on the learner's profile and represent a genuine, readable record of what they know — not how fast they learned or how they rank against others.

The course side is intentionally pressure-free. **No rankings. No competition. No leaderboards.** Courses exist to build knowledge and confidence.

---

### 2. Horse Bowl
**Purpose: Competition, speed, recall, and community.**

Horse Bowl is the platform's competitive layer — a head-to-head knowledge competition modeled on academic quiz bowl format, applied to equine science. This is where learners test their knowledge against others in a structured, sporting context.

**Gamification elements:**
- Competitive points — earned per match/round
- Rankings — individual performance tracking
- Leaderboards — visible standings within the community
- Tournaments — scheduled competitive events
- Team / Barn competitions *(future)* — group-based competition
- **Seasonal resets** — leaderboards reset each season so new learners always have a realistic path to ranking

Seasonal resets are a deliberate design choice. Permanent leaderboards create an untouchable top tier that discourages newer players from engaging competitively. Resets keep the competitive side healthy and accessible as the user base grows.

The mastery badges earned in courses may surface in Horse Bowl as credibility signals — indicating to opponents which topic areas a competitor has formally studied.

**Horse Bowl is the only competitive element of the platform.**

---

### 3. Virtual Horse
**Purpose: Care judgment, responsibility, and scenario-based learning.**

The virtual horse is the platform's most unique pillar. It is a care simulation — closer to a thoughtful management game than a traditional quiz or lesson. Learners are responsible for a virtual horse and must make real horsemanship decisions based on scenarios that reflect genuine equine care situations.

**This is not a leaderboard experience.** The virtual horse is personal. The goal is: *"Can you take thoughtful care of your horse?"* — not *"Are you better than everyone else?"*

**Scoring (personal, not comparative):**
- **Horse Health Score** — reflects physical condition based on care decisions
- **Trust Score** — reflects the relationship built through consistent, appropriate handling and care (mirrors real equine psychology)
- **Care Score** — tracks quality of daily/weekly care decisions
- **Training Progress** — reflects the horse's development over time

**Progression and unlockables:**
- Unlockable horse breeds — different breeds with breed-appropriate characteristics and care considerations
- Unlockable tack — saddles, bridles, bits, appropriate to discipline and horse type
- Unlockable grooming tools — curry combs, brushes, hoof picks, etc.
- Unlockable barn items — stall equipment, stable management tools
- Unlockable blankets — turnout, stable, coolers, appropriate to season/scenario
- Unlockable feed options — hay varieties, grain, supplements, feeding scenarios
- *(unlockable list grows with platform development)*

**Scenarios:**
- Delivered a few times per week — frequent enough to build habit, not so frequent it becomes a daily obligation
- Based on real horse care decisions — behavioral changes, lameness assessment, seasonal management, feeding decisions, weather considerations, herd dynamics
- Tiered to what the learner has unlocked — early learners get foundational scenarios, advanced learners with more unlocks face more complex situations
- Written from genuine industry expertise — scenarios reflect the judgment calls of real horse ownership, not surface-level "call the vet" decisions
- Wrong decisions have consequences — trust score drops, health score affected — creating a natural feedback loop that motivates learners to return to course content for better answers

**The connection between pillars:**
Courses build the knowledge needed to make better decisions in both Horse Bowl and the virtual horse. The virtual horse creates intrinsic motivation to study the courses. Horse Bowl rewards learners who have done the coursework. The three pillars reinforce each other without forcing any learner into a pillar they don't want.

## Content Philosophy

- **Accuracy above all** — authored by a credentialed equestrian science professional, not compiled from secondary sources
- **Depth is a differentiator** — scenarios, terminology, and content go further than anything currently available in online equine education
- **Approachable, not dumbed down** — clear language and structured progression without sacrificing accuracy
- **Visual-first** — diagrams, illustrated references, and visual identification are central to the learning method
- **Application over memorization** — the virtual horse and Horse Bowl both test judgment and reasoning, not just recall

## Your Stable
**Purpose: Personal profile and long-term journey tracking.**

The Stable is the learner's home base — a dashboard that reflects everything they've built across all three pillars in one place. It replaces the generic "points" model with meaningful, identity-based progress language.

Instead of:
> 14,250 points

A learner sees:
> 12 Courses Completed · 8 Mastery Badges Earned · 3 Horses in Your Stable · 45-Day Learning Streak · Horse Bowl Silver Division

Every number means something specific. The Stable tells the story of who this learner is and how far they've come — across courses, competition, and care.

**What lives inside the Stable:**
- Courses completed
- Mastery badges earned (organized by topic)
- Active virtual horses (with their individual scores — health, trust, care, training)
- Horse Bowl achievements and current division/ranking
- Learning streak
- Completion certificates
- Collection progress (breeds unlocked, tack collected, barn items, etc.)

**Design philosophy:**
The Stable should feel earned and personal, not like a stats dashboard. It's closer to a trophy room than an analytics page. Someone should be able to glance at another learner's Stable and immediately understand what kind of horse person they are — dedicated course completer, competitive Horse Bowl player, meticulous virtual horse caretaker, or all three.

The Stable also serves as passive motivation. Seeing courses you haven't completed, mastery badges you haven't earned, and breeds you haven't unlocked creates natural discovery and re-engagement without any notification or push mechanic required.

## Possible Landing Page Concept

The homepage has an opportunity to show prospective learners what their journey *could* look like — not just what the platform offers, but what they're building toward.

**Core concept:**
Rather than leading with features ("Interactive quizzes! Video lessons! Leaderboards!"), lead with identity. Show what a learner becomes after engaging with the platform:

> *"After 3 months on Equine Edu:"*
> 12 Courses Completed · 8 Mastery Badges · 3 Horses in Your Stable · 45-Day Streak · Horse Bowl Silver Division

This reframes the value proposition from "here's what we have" to "here's who you become." That's a fundamentally different and more compelling pitch, especially for a subscription product.

**Possible landing page section flow:**
1. **Hero** — bold headline, platform identity, single primary CTA ("Start Learning Free")
2. **The Three Pillars** — Learn. Compete. Care. — one short, visual block per pillar
3. **Your Stable preview** — show what a real learner profile looks like at 30/60/90 days in
4. **Featured course preview** — 2–3 course cards to make the content feel real and immediate
5. **Who this is for** — short, honest framing (no horse needed, all ages, all levels)
6. **Credentials block** — BSc equestrian science, 20 years industry experience, facts-based content
7. **FAQ** — 4–5 questions addressing common hesitations
8. **CTA footer** — "Start your stable today."

**Key homepage copy direction:**
- Lead with what learners *build*, not what the platform *has*
- The three-sentence product summary ("Learn through courses. Compete in Horse Bowl. Care for your virtual horse.") should appear on the homepage — it's the clearest articulation of what makes this different
- The credential block should be explicit, not vague — not "created by equine professionals" but "built by a BSc-qualified equestrian scientist with 20 years of industry experience"
- Avoid feature lists in the hero — save those for individual pillar sections

## Virtual Horse — Scenes & Poses

The virtual horse is not just a static avatar on a white background. Each breed has multiple scenes — specific environments and poses that place the horse in its actual world. Scenes make care scenarios feel real rather than abstract.

**How scenes work:**
- Each breed has multiple scene variations (turnout, stall, crossties, arena, pasture, grooming area, show environment, etc.)
- The pose and setting match the breed's natural context — a Saddlebred in a turnout scene looks different from a stock horse in a pasture scene
- Scenes are pre-generated illustrations (same OpenArt workflow as base horses) — not coded backgrounds

**Scenes connect to the scenario system:**
When a care scenario triggers, the learner sees their horse in the relevant scene. A feeding scenario triggers in the stall. A weather/turnout decision triggers in the pasture. A health observation scenario triggers at the crossties or in the barn aisle. The environment grounds the decision emotionally and makes the learning feel applied rather than theoretical.

**Scenes as unlockable progression:**
Scenes unlock as scores improve — not just cosmetic rewards but a reflection of the horse's life expanding as the learner improves their care.

Example progression:
- Start: basic stall scene
- Unlock: turnout/pasture as care score improves
- Unlock: nicer barn environment as trust score builds
- Unlock: arena or show environment at advanced stages

The environment itself communicates how well the learner is doing without needing a score to tell them — a well-cared-for horse ends up in a better world.

**Asset production notes:**
- Same base breed, different pose and environment per scene
- Consistent illustration style across all scenes (OpenArt workflow)
- Scenes are standalone images — not composited from separate layers
- Marking and tack overlays still apply on top of scene images where appropriate
