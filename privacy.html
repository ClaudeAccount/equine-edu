Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store, and protect your information — and our commitments to children's privacy.

Effective Date: June 24, 2026

Equine EDU (“Equine EDU,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) operates the website www.equineedu.com and provides educational content, courses, quizzes, and interactive learning tools focused on equine education. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share information when you use our services, including creating an account, enrolling in courses, purchasing a membership, or interacting with any part of the platform. By using Equine EDU, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information You Provide

We collect information that you voluntarily provide when you create an account, make a purchase, contact support, or participate in interactive features such as quizzes, Horse Bowl activities, or surveys. This information may include your name, email address, username, password, date of birth if provided or required for age verification, billing information processed through Stripe, learning activity data, course progress, and any additional information you choose to submit through your use of the platform.

2. Information Collected Automatically

We also collect certain information automatically when you access the platform. This includes your IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, time spent on pages, referring URLs, and usage behavior within courses and learning tools. This information is used to operate the platform, maintain security, analyze usage patterns, and improve the overall learning experience.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Equine EDU uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to keep users signed in, remember preferences, measure performance, analyze traffic, and improve content and platform functionality. You may disable cookies through your browser settings, but doing so may affect how the platform functions.

4. Learning Data

We store learning-related data in order to provide a personalized educational experience. This includes courses you have enrolled in, lesson completion status, quiz results, Horse Bowl activity, progress tracking, and any achievements or learning records associated with your account. This information is used solely to deliver educational functionality and to allow you to continue your learning experience across sessions.

5. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to create and manage accounts, process payments through Stripe, deliver educational content, track learning progress, provide customer support, send important account-related communications, improve platform functionality, and send optional marketing communications if you choose to receive them. We may also use aggregated or anonymized data for analytics and platform improvement.

6. Payments

Payments are processed securely through Stripe. Equine EDU does not store full credit or debit card numbers. Stripe may provide limited transaction information such as payment confirmation, transaction identifiers, billing details, and subscription status. Stripe acts as an independent payment processor responsible for handling sensitive payment data.

7. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with trusted third-party service providers who help operate the platform, including payment processors, hosting providers, analytics services, email services, and security providers. These providers are given only the information necessary to perform their services and are required to protect it appropriately. We may also disclose information when required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, to protect the rights and safety of users, or in connection with a business transfer such as a merger or acquisition.

8. Analytics

Equine EDU may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics to understand how users interact with the platform and to improve the learning experience. We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

9. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Equine EDU may be used by children under the supervision of a parent, guardian, school, or instructor. We comply with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Children under the age of thirteen may only use Equine EDU with verified parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under thirteen without such consent. If we discover that information has been collected without proper consent, it will be deleted promptly. Parents or guardians may request access to, correction of, or deletion of their child’s information at any time.

Parental Consent Notice. If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child under thirteen without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians may review, update, or request deletion of their child’s personal information at any time by contacting us at admin@equineedu.com.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect personal information, including secure servers, access controls, and industry-standard security practices. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain educational records such as course progress and completion history. Users may request deletion of their account at any time, subject to legal and operational requirements that may require retention of certain data.

12. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, as well as to withdraw consent for certain uses or opt out of marketing communications. Residents of certain jurisdictions, including California, may have additional privacy rights. Equine EDU does not sell personal information.

13. International Users

Your information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate. By using Equine EDU, you understand that your data may be transferred and processed outside your local jurisdiction.

14. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services or legal requirements. When updates occur, the revised policy will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, you may contact Equine EDU at www.equineedu.com or via email at admin@equineedu.com.
