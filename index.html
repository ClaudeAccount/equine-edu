About Equine Edu

Real Equine Education, Brought to You by Experts.

Equine Edu was created by real professionals working or have worked in the equine worlds. These passionate individuals helped design a program that brings you closer to understanding horses—focusing on everything from horse anatomy and behavior to breed identification and more.

This platform is not about how to ride or train horses. Instead, Equine Edu is all about learning general knowledge about horses. Any references to riding focus on the history or theory behind it, helping you gain a deeper understanding of the theories behihnd them.
