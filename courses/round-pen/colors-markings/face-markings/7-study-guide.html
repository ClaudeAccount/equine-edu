Key Concepts

Name the common face markings from narrowest to widest.

Star (small isolated forehead spot), Strip (narrow line down the bridge of the nose), Blaze (wide stripe down the center of the face), Snip (small isolated marking on the muzzle between the nostrils), Bald Face (very wide marking that may cover the eyes and muzzle).

What is a combination marking, and how is it written?

A combination marking is any face marking made up of more than one component — for example, a star with a strip below it. It is written by listing each component separately: ‘Star and Strip,’ not combined into one invented word.

What makes a marking ‘irregular,’ and how is it described?

An irregular marking has an unusual shape, interrupted outline, or unusual color distribution. It is described precisely as seen — the irregularity itself is part of the official identification.

In what direction should face markings be described?

Top to bottom: start at the poll or forehead and work down to the muzzle and chin.

Width and Location

What distinguishes a blaze from a strip?

Width. A strip is narrow and stays on the bridge of the nose. A blaze is wide and typically covers much of the face front. Both run vertically down the face, but a blaze is noticeably broader.

Where exactly does a snip appear, and how is it different from a star?

A snip is on the soft tissue of the muzzle, between or around the nostrils. A star is on the forehead. They can appear on the same horse and are listed as separate components of a combination marking.

What is a bald face, and what makes it distinctive?

A bald face is an extremely wide marking that typically covers the entire face front, often including one or both eyes and reaching down to the muzzle. It is the widest category of face marking.

Scenario Spot-Checks

A horse has a small white patch on its forehead and a separate small white patch between its nostrils. How is this described?

Star and Snip — a combination marking. Each component is named separately.

A horse has a very wide white marking that runs from between the eyes all the way down to and around the muzzle. What marking is this?

Bald Face. The width and coverage of both the face and muzzle area place it in this category.
