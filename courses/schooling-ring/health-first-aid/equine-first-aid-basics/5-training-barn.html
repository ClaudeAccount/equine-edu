Equine First Aid Basics

Training Barn

Apply what has been covered through focused practice — first aid kit identification, vital sign assessment practice, and emergency recognition scenarios.

Practice Before the Quiz

The Training Barn activities reinforce the core skills from this course: identifying first aid kit contents and their uses, reading vital signs and determining whether they fall within the normal range, and evaluating scenarios to determine whether a situation is a true emergency requiring an immediate veterinary call. Complete these activities before moving to the Study Guide.

Activity 1

First Aid Kit Contents

Review a list of supplies and identify which belong in an equine first aid kit, which are not appropriate for routine wound care, and what each essential item is used for. Reinforce the purpose of assessment tools, wound care supplies, and bandaging materials.

Open Learning Loft
Activity 2

Vital Signs Assessment

Review a series of vital sign readings — heart rate, respiratory rate, body temperature, and capillary refill time — and determine whether each reading falls within, above, or below the normal range for an adult horse at rest. Practice identifying which readings indicate an emergency.

Open Learning Loft
Activity 3

Emergency Recognition Scenarios

Work through a series of barn scenarios describing a horse's condition and behavior. For each scenario, determine whether the situation is a true emergency requiring an immediate veterinary call, a situation that warrants a call but not necessarily an emergency visit, or a minor issue that can be monitored without immediate veterinary contact.
