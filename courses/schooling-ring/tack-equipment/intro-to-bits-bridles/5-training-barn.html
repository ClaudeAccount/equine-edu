Intro to Bits & Bridles

Training Barn

Practice applying what the lessons covered — bit identification, bridle part names, and matching equipment to its function. Nothing here is scored; all activities can be repeated as many times as needed.

Practice Before the Quiz

The Training Barn activities reinforce the core content from this course: identifying bits by family and ring type, naming the parts of a snaffle bridle, and working through practical scenarios connecting equipment knowledge to barn situations. Complete these activities before moving to the Study Guide.

Activity 1

Bit Family Sort

Sort a set of bits — including loose ring snaffle, D-ring snaffle, full cheek snaffle, eggbutt snaffle, and curb bits with varying shank lengths — into the correct family. Then identify whether each acts through direct pressure or leverage.

Start Activity
Activity 2

Bridle Parts Match

Match the names of bridle parts — crownpiece, browband, cheekpieces, noseband, throatlatch, reins — to their descriptions and their locations on the bridle. Covers both English snaffle bridle parts and western bridle differences.

Start Activity
Activity 3

Equipment Scenarios

Work through short barn scenarios — a horse is showing bit resistance, a throatlatch looks too tight, a browband appears to be pinching — and use course knowledge to identify what the problem might be and what should be checked.

Start Activity
