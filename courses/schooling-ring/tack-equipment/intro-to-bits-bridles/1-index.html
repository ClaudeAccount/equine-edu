Tack & Equipment

Intro to Bits & Bridles

A bit is the primary tool of rein communication, and the bridle is what holds it in place. This course introduces the two main bit families — snaffle and curb — how each acts on the horse, and the bridle parts that every rider and handler needs to know by name.
