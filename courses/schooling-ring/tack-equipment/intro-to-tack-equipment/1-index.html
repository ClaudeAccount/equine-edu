Tack & Equipment

Introduction to Tack & Equipment

Tack is the equipment used on or around a horse for riding, driving, and safe handling. From saddles and bridles to halters and lead ropes, each piece has a specific job and helps the horse and handler work together safely. This course introduces the most common types of tack and equipment — what each item is called, how it is used, and where it belongs on the horse — and explores why proper fit is important for both comfort and safety.
