Tack & Equipment

Tack Fundamentals & Fit

Tack is the equipment used to ride, drive, and handle horses. This course explains how tack works as a connected system, why correct fit keeps a horse comfortable and safe, and how the main pieces work together — ideas that apply to every riding style.
