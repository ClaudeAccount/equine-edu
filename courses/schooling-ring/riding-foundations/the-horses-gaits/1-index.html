Riding Foundations

The Horse's Gaits

Every horse moves in four natural gaits: the walk, trot, canter, and gallop. Each gait has its own footfall pattern, rhythm, and speed. Learning how horses move helps riders stay balanced, give clearer signals, and notice what the horse is doing.
