Horse Care

Measuring Horses

Knowing a horse's height and weight is an important part of horse care. This course explains how horses are measured using hands, how to correctly measure height at the withers, and common ways to estimate or weigh a horse's body weight. Learners will also discover why accurate measurements matter for health, feeding, equipment fit, transportation, and competition.
