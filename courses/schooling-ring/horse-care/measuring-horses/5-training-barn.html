Measuring Horses

Training Barn

Apply what has been covered in the lessons through focused practice activities — notation conversion, hands-to-inches calculation, and identifying the withers and measuring equipment.

Practice Before the Quiz

The Training Barn provides activities that reinforce the core skills from this course: reading and writing measurement notation correctly, converting between hands and inches, and applying measurement knowledge to practical scenarios. Complete these activities before moving to the Study Guide.

Activity 1

Notation Practice

Read a series of horse height measurements written in hand notation and identify which are correctly written and which contain notation errors. Practice converting measurements between hands-and-inches notation and total-inch values.

Start Activity
Activity 2

Hands-to-Inches Converter

Practice converting height values between hands notation and total inches. Work through a series of conversion problems that reinforce the relationship between the hand unit and standard inch measurements — and identify where the pony threshold falls.

Start Activity
Activity 3

Measurement Scenarios

Review a set of measuring scenarios and identify what is correct or incorrect about each setup: the measuring point used, the horse's stance, the surface type, and equipment choice. Apply the course content to recognize errors before they affect a measurement.

Start Activity
