From Hoof Wall to Coffin Bone

The course is split into short lessons. Each one builds on the last. You will learn by reading, looking at the hoof, and practicing what you learn.

You can learn at your own pace. Each lesson uses simple words, ties into Horse Bowl questions, and shows you how to use what you learn at the barn.

Perfect For
