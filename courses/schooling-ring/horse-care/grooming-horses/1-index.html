What This Course Teaches

Grooming is the daily practice of cleaning, inspecting, and caring for a horse's coat, skin, mane, tail, and hooves. This course builds a complete foundation: the health and bonding reasons behind routine grooming, the tools used and how to select them, safe technique for each area of the horse, and hoof picking as an essential part of every grooming session.
