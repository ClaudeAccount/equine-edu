Horse Care

Grooming Horses

Grooming is a daily care routine that keeps horses clean, healthy, and comfortable. It is also a chance to check the horse's body for cuts, swelling, or other changes. This course covers the grooming tools, how each is used, safe habits, the correct order, and why hoof picking is needed every session.
