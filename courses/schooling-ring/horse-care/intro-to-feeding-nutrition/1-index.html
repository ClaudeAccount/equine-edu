What This Course Teaches

The horse's digestive system is sensitive to changes in feed type, schedule, and amount. This course introduces the principles that make equine feeding management work: the role of forage as the dietary foundation, the importance of consistent schedules and gradual changes, water requirements, and the practical routines that keep horses healthy at the feed level.
