Intro to Feeding & Nutrition

Training Barn

Apply what has been covered in the lessons through focused practice — forage identification, feeding schedule scenarios, and water and feed management decisions.

Practice Before the Quiz

The Training Barn activities reinforce the core content from this course: identifying forage types and evaluating quality, applying the core feeding principles to realistic scenarios, and making water and feed management decisions. Complete these activities before moving to the Study Guide.

Activity 1

Forage Identification

Review descriptions of different forage types and characteristics. Identify each forage type, determine whether hay samples meet quality standards, and match forage types to appropriate horse types based on nutritional needs and workload.

Start Activity
Activity 2

Feeding Schedule Scenarios

Review a set of feeding management scenarios and evaluate each one against the core feeding principles from the course: consistent schedules, gradual change, forage availability, and appropriate grain management. Identify what each scenario gets right and what it gets wrong.

Start Activity
Activity 3

Water and Feed Management Decisions

Work through a series of water and daily feed management situations — evaluate water sources, determine appropriate water availability practices, assess feeding routines for common errors, and apply the health observation habits covered in the course.

Start Activity
