The Viewing Room

Review the major Appaloosa pattern families before moving into active practice.

Look for the Main Pattern Clue

Use this room as a quiet study step. The goal is not to label every possible variation, but to recognize the strongest clue first: hip blanket, body spots, roaning, or subtle Appaloosa characteristics.

Hip Pattern

Blanket

Start at the hips. A blanket is a white or light area over the rump, with or without spots inside it.

Spotted Body

Leopard

Leopard patterns have white spread over much of the body with dark spots distributed through the coat.

Light Flecks

Snowflake

Snowflake describes white spots or flecks on a darker coat. They may become easier to see as the horse matures.

Roaning

Varnish Roan

Varnish roan often lightens over time while darker varnish marks remain around bony areas.

Study tip: if a horse seems to fit more than one Appaloosa term, describe the strongest pattern first and then add any additional visible clues.
