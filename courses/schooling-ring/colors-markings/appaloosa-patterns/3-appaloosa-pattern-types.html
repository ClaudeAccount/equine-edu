Appaloosa Patterns

Appaloosa Pattern Types

Study common Appaloosa pattern names in groups: blanket patterns, spotted-body patterns, and roaning or subtle expressions.

Pattern Names Describe the Look

Appaloosa pattern terms are visual descriptions. Many horses show combinations, and some do not fit perfectly into one neat category. Use these names as clear starting points when describing a horse's pattern.

Blanket patterns show a lighter or white area over the hips or rump. The blanket may be plain or spotted.
Hip Pattern

Blanket

A white or light area over the hips or rump with little or no spotting inside the white area.

Hip Pattern

Spotted Blanket

A blanket over the hips that contains dark spots inside the white area.

Related Term

Roan Blanket

A lighter, roaned area over the hips rather than a sharply white blanket. It may have spots or only a frosted look.

Small Area

Frosted or Laced Hip

A subtle lightening or lacy white appearance over the hip area, often much less dramatic than a full blanket.

Leopard-type patterns spread white and spots over more of the horse's body.
Classic

Leopard

A mostly white coat with dark spots distributed over much of the body.

Less White

Near Leopard

Similar to leopard, but with more base coat color remaining on areas such as the head, neck, legs, or flanks.

Minimal Spots

Few-Spot Leopard

A mostly white horse with very few visible dark spots. Darker shading may remain around bony areas or protected areas.

Light Spots

Snowflake

White spots or flecks on a darker body. Snowflake patterns may become more visible as the horse ages.

Some Appaloosa expressions look more roaned or mottled than spotted, and may change gradually over time.
Changing Pattern

Varnish Roan

A leopard-complex roaning pattern that often lightens with age while darker varnish marks remain over bony areas.

Marbled Look

Marble

A mottled or marbled mixture of lighter and darker hair, often connected with leopard-complex expression.

Minimal Expression

Solid with Characteristics

Some Appaloosas may look mostly solid but still show Appaloosa characteristics such as mottled skin, striped hooves, or white sclera.

Combination

Mixed Expressions

Real horses may combine a blanket, spots, roaning, or varnish marks, so descriptions sometimes use more than one term.
