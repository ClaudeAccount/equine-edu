Leg Markings

Leg Marking Memory Game

Flip pairs of cards to match each leg marking illustration to its marking name. Find all six pairs to finish.

How to Play

The grid has 12 face-down cards: six leg marking illustrations and their six matching name cards. Flip two at a time. If they match, they stay revealed. If not, they flip back. Complete all six pairs with the fewest moves.
