The Viewing Room

Review the major pinto pattern families before moving into active practice.

Compare Where the White Appears

Use this room to slow down and compare the common pinto pattern clues. Look at the topline, legs, face, eyes, edge shape, and whether the horse seems to show one pattern family or a combination.

Topline White

Tobiano

Tobiano white often crosses the topline and forms rounded, distinct patches. Leg white is common.

Irregular White

Overo

Overo is a broad group. White may look irregular, splashy, or framed and often does not cross the topline like tobiano.

Dipped Look

Splash White

Splash white can look as if the horse was dipped in white paint, often with broad face white, high leg white, belly white, and blue eyes.

Combination

Tovero

Tovero horses show tobiano plus overo-type clues. They may have dark color around the ears, forehead, eyes, or mouth.

Study tip: name the likely pattern family first, then explain the clue that supports it.
