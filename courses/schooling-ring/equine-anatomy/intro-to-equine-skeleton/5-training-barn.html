Intro to the Equine Skeleton

Training Barn

Apply what has been covered through focused practice — bone identification, joint location, and skeletal region sorting. Nothing here is scored; all activities can be repeated as many times as needed.

Practice Before the Quiz

The Training Barn activities reinforce the core content from this course: identifying the major bones of the equine skeleton and where each is located, sorting joints into forelimb and hindlimb groups, and answering practical scenarios that connect skeletal knowledge to real barn situations. Complete these activities before moving to the Study Guide.

Activity 1

Bone Match

Match bone names — scapula, humerus, radius, carpus, cannon bone, femur, tibia, tarsus, and others — to their location and the region of the skeleton they belong to. Build recognition of the major named bones in both the axial and appendicular skeleton.

Open Learning Loft
Activity 2

Joint Sort

Sort the major joints of the horse — shoulder, elbow, carpus, fetlock, hip, stifle, hock, pastern, and coffin joint — into forelimb and hindlimb groups. Then match each joint to the bones it connects and the human equivalent joint where applicable.

Open Learning Loft
Activity 3

Skeleton Scenarios

Work through a series of practical scenarios — a veterinarian describes a finding, a horse shows swelling in a specific location, a buyer asks about bone quality — and use skeletal knowledge to identify what region or structure is being discussed and why it matters.
