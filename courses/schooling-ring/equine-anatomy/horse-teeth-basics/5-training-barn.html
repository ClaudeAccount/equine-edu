Horse Teeth Basics

Training Barn

Apply what has been covered through focused practice — tooth type identification, dental feature matching, and age estimation exercises. Nothing here is scored; all activities can be repeated as many times as needed.

Practice Before the Quiz

The Training Barn activities reinforce the core content from this course: identifying the different types of equine teeth and their locations, matching dental features to the correct tooth type, and using known age indicators to estimate a horse's approximate age. Complete these activities before moving to the Study Guide.

Activity 1

Tooth Type Match

Match each tooth type — incisors, cheek teeth, canines, and wolf teeth — to its correct description, location in the mouth, and primary function. Reinforce the differences between tooth categories and their roles in chewing and bit fit.

Open Learning Loft
Activity 2

Dental Features Sort

Sort a list of dental terms and features — cups, infundibulum, dental stars, Galvayne's groove, caps, floating, quidding — into the correct categories: incisor features, cheek tooth features, or age indicators. Build fluency with the vocabulary before the quiz.

Open Learning Loft
Activity 3

Age Estimation Scenarios

Work through a series of dental descriptions and determine the approximate age range of the horse described. Each scenario presents the state of the incisors — which teeth are temporary, which permanent teeth have erupted, whether cups are present, and whether Galvayne's groove is visible — and asks for an age estimate based on the known timeline.
