Equine Anatomy

Expanding on Anatomy

This course builds on the foundations course by adding more detailed vocabulary for the visible parts of the physical horse.

Developing Your Eye

In the foundations course, you learned to identify the major areas of the horse. This course builds on that foundation by breaking down those larger areas into common terms for exact body parts.

Horses are big animals, and they have a lot of body to name!

What You Will Learn

The foundations course introduced the major regions. This course goes deeper into each one.

Region 1

Head

You already know where the head is. Now you will learn every specific term — the poll, forelock, throatlatch, jaw, chin groove, and all the features between the ears and the muzzle.

Region 2

Neck & Shoulder

The crest, jugular groove, point of shoulder, and chest — terms that come up constantly when describing how a horse looks and moves.

Region 3

The Body

The back, loin, croup, barrel, flank, heart girth, and more — the body region has more named structures than most people expect.

Region 4

The Legs

From elbow and stifle down to the hoof, the legs have a dense vocabulary — every joint, bony landmark, and surface structure on both front and hind legs.
