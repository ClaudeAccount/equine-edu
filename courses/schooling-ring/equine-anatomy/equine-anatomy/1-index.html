Horse Anatomy

Parts of the Horse

This course covers the full visible anatomy learners need to memorize and recognize on the physical horse. You'll learn the names of the main body parts, how they are identified, and what they look like in real horses. The content is organized by region: head, neck and shoulder, body, front legs, and hind legs.
