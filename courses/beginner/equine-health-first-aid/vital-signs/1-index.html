Course Focus

Check, Understand, and Act on Vital Signs

This course introduces the six essential equine vital signs through guided lessons, step-by-step technique descriptions, scenario-based practice, and interactive activities. Learners develop the ability to take accurate readings, understand what those numbers mean, and recognize when something needs veterinary attention.

✓ Know the normal range for each vital sign
✓ Understand how to check each vital sign correctly
✓ Recognize readings that fall outside the normal range
✓ Perform the skin tenting and capillary refill tests
✓ Know what each vital sign tells you about a horse's health
✓ Feel confident communicating findings to a veterinarian
