Course Focus

Name and Locate Every Major Body Part

This course introduces the parts of the horse through organized visual lessons, an interactive viewing room, and skill-building games. Learners develop the ability to correctly name and locate key body parts on the head, neck and shoulders, body, and legs.

✓ Name the major parts of the horse's head
✓ Identify the neck, shoulder, and withers
✓ Locate the key parts of the horse's body
✓ Name the front and hind leg parts from top to hoof
✓ Understand why each term is used in everyday horse care
✓ Use correct terminology with confidence in any horse setting
